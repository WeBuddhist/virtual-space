# Asset credits

Everything else in WeBuddhist Space is made in code (procedural geometry, sculpted
statues in `scripts/statues`, synthesized sound).

## Animation

- **Motion capture:** idle, formal walk and seated meditation clips from the
  [Mesh2Motion](https://github.com/Mesh2Motion/mesh2motion-app) human animation library
  (which includes Quaternius's Universal Animation Library), **CC0 1.0**. They are retargeted
  onto our avatar's skeleton by `scripts/mocap/build.ts` into `public/animations/mocap.json`.
  No credit is required; we give it gladly.
- **Bows and prostrations** (Tibetan full prostration, Thai five-point bow, East Asian kneeling
  bow, standing bow), palms together and the mala are hand-keyed in `src/avatar.ts`.

To rebuild the clips, download the `.glb` files listed in `scripts/mocap/build.ts` from
`static/assets/human` in the Mesh2Motion repository into a folder, then run
`npm run mocap -- <folder>`.

## Statues

- **The Bodh Gaya image** (`public/models/statues/bodhgaya.spz`) is a Gaussian splat of the
  main image in the Mahabodhi sanctum. It was generated by the WeBuddhist team with an
  image-to-3D model on Hugging Face, then cleaned (floaters and baked-in glass glare removed),
  stood upright and scaled by `scripts/statues/splat_clean.py`, and compressed with
  `scripts/statues/spz.ts` (Spark's SPZ encoder). It is drawn with
  [Spark](https://sparkjs.dev) (MIT). The sculpted statue from `scripts/statues/build.ts` is
  the fallback if the splat cannot load.

The images in the other three rooms are made from photographs on Wikimedia Commons: an
image-to-3D reconstruction (TRELLIS on Hugging Face, as Gaussian splats for the three main
images) or a monocular depth estimate (Depth Anything V2, run locally) for the photo reliefs,
cut out, cleaned and coloured from the photo. Each model is an
adaptation of its photographs and is shared under the same licence (CC BY-SA where the
photo is CC BY-SA).

## Instrument sounds

The singing bowl (struck and sung), drilbu bell, damaru, drum, cymbals, gong, temple
bell, wood fish and dharma drum are real recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org)
under CC0 1.0, CC BY 3.0 and CC BY 4.0, trimmed and loudness-normalised. Authors,
licenses, source pages and the edits made are listed in
[`public/audio/instruments/SOURCES.md`](../../public/audio/instruments/SOURCES.md), which
ships with the site and is linked from the settings sheet. The synthesized voices in
`src/audio.ts` remain the fallback.

## Chant recordings

- **Bodh Gaya's chant** (`public/audio/bodhgaya/daily-chanting-mahabodhi.mp3`) is the
  audio of ["Daily Chanting at Bodhgaya Mahabodhi Temple"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlZQVNnJneI)
  by the BodhGaya Events channel on YouTube (YouTube standard license), extracted and
  loudness-normalised. It plays on "Listen to chanting" and at the dawn and evening
  services in the Bodh Gaya room.
- The other rooms' chants are the first 20 minutes of YouTube recordings (YouTube standard
  license), extracted to MP3:
  - Namdroling (`public/audio/tibetan/drubchen-puja-namdroling.mp3`):
    ["2024 Drubchen Puja at Namdroling Monastery South India"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esSPYfN3tHY)
    by KD Memory Clips.
  - Wat Phra Si Rattana Mahathat (`public/audio/theravada/evening-chanting-chinnarat.mp3`):
    ["The Wat Phra Ratana Mahathat Monks chanting Phitsanulok Thailand"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xkcHShLeprk)
    by fully being.
  - Tōdai-ji (`public/audio/mahayana/shunie-chant-todaiji.mp3`):
    ["Buddhist Chant Of Shuni-e Ceremony, Todai-Ji"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7--DJFg-C0k)
    by Yamabiko Factory.

## The Bodhi leaf

The Bodhi tree's leaves (canopy and falling leaves) use the outline and image of
["A leaf of pipal"](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_leaf_of_pipal.jpg) by
Hari gurung77, CC BY-SA 4.0, traced and warped by `scripts/foliage/bodhi_leaf.py` (the
drip tip, cropped in the photo, is extended to its usual length).
`public/textures/bodhgaya/bodhi-leaf.jpg` and `src/kit/peepal-leaf.ts` are adaptations
under the same license.
