# Theravada room: generated reference views and textures

> Reference views (`view-*.jpg`) live in `docs/rooms/theravada/views/` so they are not shipped to visitors; room textures are in `public/textures/theravada/`.

Temple: Wat Phra Si Rattana Mahathat ("Wat Yai"), Phitsanulok; Phra Buddha Chinnarat in the west-facing viharn. Source: [reference.md](reference.md).

Files are in `public/textures/theravada/`. Prompts live in `scripts/textures/sets/theravada.ts`; regenerate with `npm run textures -- theravada [name…]`.
Images were made with Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image). Gemini 4 Argon wrote the prompts from reference.md plus a few freely licensed Wikimedia Commons photos (reference.md section 8: #1, #7, #11, #12, #13, #14, #16, #17, #18, #19; used only as visual comparison, not stored in the repo), then critiqued every image against the reference text and photos.

`view-*` files are visual targets for the 3D build and are **not shipped**. Everything else is a room texture. The scene has **no surroundings** (user decision): no skyline, town, river or other monastery buildings; only the viharn, the prang directly behind it, lawn and open sky.

Budget: 30 generation requests used in total (2 rounds; 27 returned images, 3 were blocked by the model with IMAGE_RECITATION). Scores are Argon's closeness to the real place, 1–10.

## Notes for the 3D build

- **What the photos showed that the reference text understates.** The aureole is a slim arch that hugs the body, not a wide arch with its own dark panel. The two naga bodies rise beside the knees, flare at shoulder or neck height into crescent hooks, and close in a tall pointed flame-tongue arch above the finial. The "dark inner field" is the whole **black lacquer back wall** behind the image: gold flower motifs in a staggered grid, two small flying gold celestials in the upper corners, and a gilded carved valance across the top. Build it as a wall (`backdrop.jpg`) plus a separate alpha-cut arch (`aureole.jpg`).
- **Columns** are black and gold over their whole height, from base to capital, with gilded ring bands and gilded lotus-bud capitals. Crystal chandeliers hang in the nave. The ceiling is dark red with dark wooden beams and gilt trim. The nave floor is dark glossy encaustic tile, with the red carpet only in the kneeling area.
- **West front.** Four white round porch columns with gilded capitals carry a **low separate porch roof**. The main gable (gilt carving with blue-glass panels and a chofa) rises behind and above that roof, with a single central doorway inside the porch. White lidded urns on gilt bases and stone lions stand at the front. The gilded prang (dark grey stucco lower body, gilded upper third, trident finial) rises directly behind the ridge. There is **only one prang**: the first approach view wrongly showed two.
- **Ignore the cloister wings and colonnades** that appear in `view-exterior` and `view-approach`. The model kept adding them, and the room has no surroundings. Also ignore the extra porch column in `view-exterior`. In `view-approach` the main roof spreads too wide: the real hall is long and narrow (about 38 × 10 m wall line), and its tiers step down toward the entrance gable.
- **Guardians.** The views show tall standing warrior figures. The reference gives two small bronze figures, about 0.8 m tall, on the front step at the knees. Follow the reference.
- **Devotees** in `view-devotees` mostly sit back on their heels. That is right for the bow, but between bows the *phap phiab* side-saddle sit (feet pointing away from the image) should be used.
- **Texture usage caveats:**
  - `ceiling.jpg` has one large central coffer, so map one copy per bay rather than tiling it.
  - `aureole.jpg`, `gable.jpg` and `bargeboard.jpg` sit on black, so cut them out with a luminance or alpha mask.
  - In `bargeboard.jpg` the naga head points back toward the ridge, so mirror it so the head sits at the eave end.
  - `roof-tiles.jpg` mixes rounded and pointed tile tips and has a visible seam; consider a procedural or CC0 tile if it repeats noticeably.
  - `prang-gilt.jpg` (Buddha niches) and `backdrop.jpg` (busy star rosettes) are the weakest textures; the prompts in this file record what to fix next time.
- No sema stones appear anywhere; the viharn has none.
